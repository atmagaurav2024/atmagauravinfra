Company Profile

A Legacy of Excellence in Construction

Atmagaurav Infra Private Limited is a distinguished infrastructure development company based in Maharashtra, India. We specialize in the execution of complex government infrastructure projects including national highways, state highways, major bridges, and critical road safety works.

Operating under the mandate of NHAI and MoRTH, our projects span critical corridors across Maharashtra and beyond, contributing meaningfully to India's connectivity and economic growth.

Our team brings together certified engineers, experienced site managers, and a committed workforce — all aligned to one purpose: delivering infrastructure that stands the test of time.

What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive infrastructure services built on technical excellence and government-grade quality standards.

🛣

Highway Construction

We execute full-scale construction of national and state highways, employing modern construction techniques, certified materials, and stringent quality control to meet NHAI and IRC standards.

From earthworks and sub-base preparation to bituminous surfacing and road furniture installation — our team manages every phase with precision.

  • Earthwork, embankment and sub-base construction
  • Granular and bituminous pavement layers
  • Drainage structures and culverts
  • Road furniture, signage and markings
  • Quality assurance and third-party testing
🔧

Highway Maintenance

We provide scheduled and emergency maintenance on national highways under concession and NHAI maintenance contracts — ensuring road safety and structural integrity year-round.

Our maintenance teams are stationed along the corridors we manage, enabling rapid response to pothole repairs, storm damage, and routine upkeep.

  • Pothole repairs and surface patching
  • Crack sealing and overlay works
  • Drainage cleaning and restoration
  • Road marking and delineator replacement
  • Emergency response and incident management
🏛

Bridge Construction & Maintenance

Atmagaurav Infra has the technical capability to design, build, and maintain bridges — from foot over bridges serving urban communities to major road-over-bridges on national highway corridors.

  • Foot over bridges (FOBs) and pedestrian crossings
  • Road over bridges (ROBs) and underpasses
  • Minor bridge and culvert construction
  • Structural health monitoring and maintenance
  • Waterproofing and protective treatments
⚠

Road Safety Works

We execute comprehensive road safety infrastructure as mandated by NHAI and MoRTH — protecting the lives of commuters on some of India's busiest highway corridors.

  • Crash barriers — W-beam, thrie-beam, and concrete
  • Overhead gantry signs and variable message signs
  • Road studs, delineators and retro-reflective markers
  • Rumble strips and speed calming measures
  • Road safety audits and black spot rectification
